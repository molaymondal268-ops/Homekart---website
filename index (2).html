Frequently Asked Questions

How can I order products?

Add items to your cart and tap "Send order on WhatsApp". WhatsApp opens with your list ready. Press Send to share it with us.

Do you offer home delivery?

We offer nearby home delivery. Availability and delivery charges depend on your location, so please confirm with the shop. Same-day delivery is not offered.

Which areas do you deliver to?

We serve Thakdari and nearby areas in Kolkata. Send your locality with your order and we will confirm if we can deliver.

Can I collect my order from the shop?

Yes. In-store pickup and kerbside pickup are available. Choose "Pickup" when you send your order.

How can I check product availability?

Stock changes through the day. Use the "Ask on WhatsApp" button on any product, and we will confirm availability.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Cash and Google Pay. Please do not send payment until HomeKart confirms your order and total.

How can I contact HomeKart?

WhatsApp or call +91 90512 47072, 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM, every day. Or visit us at Thakdari Road, Lohapool Market.
